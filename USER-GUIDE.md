# PocketMoney User Guide

A quick tour of everything the app can do. PocketMoney has three tabs: **Expenses & Add**, **Monthly History & Summary** and **Settings**.

## Your first minute

1. Tap **Set Budget** in the header and enter your budget for the month.
2. On **Expenses & Add**, pick a category, type what you bought, enter the amount and press **Enter** (or tap the add button).
3. Watch the dashboard update: spent, remaining balance and the progress bar.

## The dashboard

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Monthly budget | The amount you set for this month |
| Total spent | Everything logged this month |
| Remaining balance | Budget (plus any money you added) minus spent |
| Budget utilization | Progress bar with the percentage of your budget used |
| 💡 insight line | Days left, safe-to-spend per day, average spend per day, what you spent today and your top category |

You get a warning when you reach 85% of your budget and another when you go over.

## Logging expenses

- **Categories:** tap one on the category bar. The app remembers the last one you used.
- **Suggestions:** as you type an item name, past items are suggested. Pick one and the amount fills in with what you paid last time.
- **Quick Add buttons** (+RM5, +RM10, +RM20, +RM50) add to the amount box so you can build up a price fast.
- **Quick options:** fill in an item and amount, then tap **Save as quick option**. It becomes a one-tap chip. Tap the ✕ on a chip to remove it.
- **Parking:** choose **Indoor** (fixed RM5.60) or **Outdoor** (type the fee).

## Managing transactions

Each transaction has three buttons:

- 🔁 **Repeat** adds the same item again with today's date
- ✏️ **Edit** changes the name, amount or category
- 🗑️ **Delete** removes it, with an **Undo** option right after

Use the search box and the filter dropdown to find things. The filter also has an **Added money** option.

## Adding money

If extra money comes in during the month, use the add money button in the header. It raises the amount you have available and shows as "includes +RM X added" on the dashboard.

## Categories

Tap the **＋** at the end of the category bar to open the category manager.

- **On your bar:** categories you can use now. Tap **Remove** to hide one (at least one must stay).
- **Removed:** hidden categories, with a **+ Add** button to bring them back. Your history is kept.
- **More to add:** presets such as Utilities, Transport, Fun and Rent.
- **Create your own:** choose a name and an emoji.

## Monthly history and summary

- Pick any month from the archive dropdown.
- See the budget, total spent and money saved for that month.
- The doughnut chart shows spending by category, and the list shows that month's transactions.

At the start of a new month the app archives the old one and starts fresh automatically.

## Settings

- **Profile:** name, profile picture, wallpaper.
- **Preferences:** accent colour and currency.
- **Data Backup:** Cloud Sync, Export JSON, Export CSV and Import JSON.

## Backups

- **Export JSON** saves everything (transactions, archives, budget, settings). Use it before switching devices or clearing browser data.
- **Export CSV** gives you a spreadsheet of your transactions.
- **Import JSON** restores a backup.

Without cloud sync, your data lives only in your browser, so back up now and then.
